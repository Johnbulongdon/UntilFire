# SEO audit — 24 September 2026

Baseline: `511c435`. Read alongside [Search Console setup](search-console.md)
and the [roadmap](ROADMAP.md). Private analytics counts are intentionally not
copied into this public repository.

## Evidence and scope

The production Search Console sync succeeded on September 24, with data through
September 21. Totals span roughly three months; page/query detail spans only
September 11–21. These different windows must not be compared as equivalent.
The stored page and query dimensions are separate, not a query-to-page join.
Sparse query rows do not account for every click. Search clicks are not signups.
Use impression-weighted positions and clicks / impressions, not averages of
daily ratios, when summarising a period.

Existing savings-rate and Singapore pages have some first-page visibility,
but the sample is too small to establish that their snippets cause low clicks.
The savings-rate page was selected because live inspection also found concrete
content issues: competing main headings, fixed timeline claims inconsistent with
the interactive results, and unclear annual/monthly and payroll conventions.

## First bounded improvement

- Make savings-rate metadata describe the percentage calculation and estimated
  years to FIRE, without promising an exact retirement date.
- Keep the calculator's main heading and make the supporting explanation a
  secondary heading. State the formula with an annual-input worked example.
- Explain the existing model's assumptions and starting-balance dependence;
  remove conflicting fixed retirement timelines. Keep visible FAQ and JSON-LD
  generated from the same answers, including period and payroll limitations.
- Retain the existing links into the full FIRE calculator and learning content.
  No formulas, defaults, account flows or tracking behavior change.

Google's [title guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/title-link)
and [snippet guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/snippet)
support clear, descriptive content; Google may choose different search text.
This is a content-quality improvement and a measurable hypothesis, not a claim
that headings or metadata guarantee rankings or clicks.

## Preserve existing decisions

`9908ea5` deliberately excluded APY and compound-interest tools from indexing.
`40db7ff` expanded Coast FIRE content on September 21. Preserve those choices
and allow time to measure the recent change; earlier impressions do not show
its effect. Do not reopen city URL migrations, build more city pages, or
automatically integrate old SEO PRs from this audit. Singapore remains a
candidate for a separate intent/content review, not a proven quick win.

## Verification and follow-up

Production build, existing SEO and FIRE projection guards, focused ESLint, and
diff whitespace checks passed. Browser inspection confirmed the live baseline,
updated title/canonical, one h1, six FAQ entries, unchanged example calculation,
and no horizontal overflow at 390px and 1280px. Light/mobile and dark/desktop
screenshots were inspected. Application-wide lint warnings predate this scope.

After Claude integrates and confirms deployment, record the live commit/date.
Compare a full 28-day post-publication window with the preceding 28 days, allowing
for Search Console's reporting delay. Track page impressions, clicks, CTR and
query mix; low counts mean results are directional, not causal proof. Verify
organic landing → calculator completion → signup tracking before claiming user
growth. Do not equate improved search position with retention or revenue.
