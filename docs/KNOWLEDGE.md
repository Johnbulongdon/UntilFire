## Preserve the reasoning with the change

Before a material product, design, architecture, or workflow change, read the
relevant active decision and its rationale. If the request overturns it, explain
the conflict and record the authorized replacement. Do not restart an old debate
just because a new agent or session did not see its history.

A decision entry records date, status (active/proposed/superseded/historical),
decision, why, rejected alternatives/trade-offs, source/evidence, and conditions
for revisiting it. Link both ways when superseding a decision. A proposal is not
approval. If the historical reason is unknown, say so rather than inventing it.

In the same PR, update the current contract, decision log when a meaningful
choice changes, changelog for implementation, and roadmap status when relevant.
Routine fixes do not need a new decision essay. Report what was verified and
what remains unknown. Keep one maintained explanation per topic; link to it.

## Historical material and privacy

Older sprint plans, audits, market research, personas, launch copy and module
snapshots preserve context. Their original checkboxes, prices, feature lists,
and approval labels are not current requirements or permission to execute.
Use the owners above when they conflict. Historical design proposals are marked
at their entry points; do not restore Monte Carlo, daily streaks, old palettes,
or old navigation from them.

The app repository is public. Keep raw feedback, respondent portfolios, contact
lists, private analytics/account details, credentials, and personal notes out of
it. Preserve safe conclusions with source dates and limitations. Retaining such
source material privately does not make it a second operational instruction set.
No routine vault reading or dual writing is required.
