  first page in the stored query sample. Some location pages appear nearer the
  first page but have very few impressions. This is not evidence that the entire
  site ranks well or that a snippet is causing low CTR.
- Totals cover a longer window than page/query detail. As checked September 25,
  stored data ends September 21, with detail beginning September 11. Page and
  query reports are separate; do not join them by guesswork. Reporting lag and
  recent releases prevent a post-change ranking conclusion today.

  are not a demonstrated missing ingredient. A large open editorial backlog is
  not a reason to mass-publish it.

  Replace that obsolete destination with the existing free FIRE estimate and a

## Blogs and budget

Use supporting articles when a specific unanswered question connects to a useful
tool. Avoid a generic post quota, year-stamped duplicates, large city expansions
and bulk publishing. Preserve existing noindex decisions for off-focus tools.

No evidence currently makes paid SEO tooling or advertising a prerequisite.
Search Console and the existing analytics can guide the next work, subject to
their access and coverage limits. Advertising can buy visits but would be a
separate, explicitly budgeted acquisition experiment; it is not proof of improved
organic ranking. Do not promise top-ten rankings or a date for traffic growth.

## Self-review and stop conditions

Before every handoff: verify current main and overlapping PRs, inspect the full
diff, check supported claims and real destinations, validate relevant output and
mobile/desktop behavior, preserve decision history and state measurement limits.
Do not churn a recently published title based on a few days of noisy data.
Fix demonstrated defects immediately; evaluate growth using complete 28-day
windows with reporting lag and internal traffic excluded. Revisit priority if
clicks grow but calculator completion or signup does not.
